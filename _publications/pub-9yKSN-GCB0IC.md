<p class="lang-zh">中国发明专利，2024 年 10 月 1 日授权公告（授权公告号 CN117504737B）。将特斯拉阀（无动件单向阀）引入流化床反应器结构设计，利用其正反向流阻差异强化气固接触与颗粒循环。专利权人：天韵低碳新能源科技(辽宁)有限责任公司。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Chinese invention patent granted on October 1, 2024 (publication No. CN117504737B). A Tesla valve (moving-part-free check valve) is introduced into the fluidized-bed reactor design, exploiting its direction-dependent flow resistance to enhance gas-solid contact and particle circulation. Assignee: Tianyun Low Carbon New Energy Technology (Liaoning) Co., Ltd.</p>
