<p class="lang-zh">中国发明专利申请（申请号 CN202111566075.2，公布号 CN114329824A，2022-04-12 公布）。基于水力采集头内部流场特性，采用拉格朗日方法计算结核运动轨迹，得到采集头内部参数与采集车运行速度等参数的匹配关系，为采集车设计提供依据。专利权人：中国地质大学(北京)。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Chinese invention patent application (Appl. No. CN202111566075.2; publication No. CN114329824A, published Apr 12, 2022). Based on the internal flow field of the hydraulic collection head, the Lagrange method is adopted to compute nodule trajectories, deriving the matching relationship between head parameters and collector-vehicle operating speed. Assignee: China University of Geosciences (Beijing).</p>
