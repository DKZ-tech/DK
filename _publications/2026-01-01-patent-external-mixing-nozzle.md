<p class="lang-zh">中国发明专利申请（申请号 CN202610181380.6，申请日 2026-02-09），2026 年 4 月 14 日申请公布。外混式双流体雾化喷枪通过气体导流盘形成旋转气流，气液两相在喷嘴外部剪切混合实现高效雾化，不易堵塞、维护便捷（申请阶段）。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Chinese invention patent application (Appl. No. CN202610181380.6, filed Feb 9, 2026), published Apr 14, 2026. An external-mixing dual-fluid atomizing spray gun with a gas guide disc forming swirling flow; gas and liquid mix and shear outside the nozzle for high-efficiency atomization with low clogging risk (patent application stage).</p>
