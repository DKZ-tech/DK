<p class="lang-zh">发表于《矿冶工程》2023 年第 43 卷第 3 期。通过水槽试验研究深海多金属结核采集过程对海底沉积物的扰动规律，为评估深海采矿的环境影响提供试验依据。DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.0253-6099.2023.03.005。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Published in Mining and Metallurgical Engineering (Vol. 43, No. 3, 2023). Flume experiments were conducted to study the disturbance of seabed sediments during deep-sea polymetallic nodule collection, providing experimental evidence for environmental impact assessment of deep-sea mining. DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.0253-6099.2023.03.005.</p>
