<p class="lang-zh">中国发明专利申请（申请号 CN202610181384.4，申请日 2026-02-09），2026 年 5 月 5 日申请公布。双喷枪互为备用的自清洁喷雾系统：一支喷枪工作时，另一支通过反向气液流自动清除堵塞物，实现喷雾连续、堵塞自排、免维护干预（申请阶段）。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Chinese invention patent application (Appl. No. CN202610181384.4, filed Feb 9, 2026), published May 5, 2026. A dual-spray-gun self-cleaning system in which the two guns back each other up: while one gun sprays, the other is automatically cleaned by reverse gas-liquid flow, enabling continuous spraying and clog self-clearing (patent application stage).</p>
