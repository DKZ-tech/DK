<p class="lang-zh">发表于《海洋技术学报》2022 年第 41 卷第 4 期。基于标准 k-ε 湍流模型对双排喷嘴射流式采集头内部流场进行数值模拟，匹配射流角度、喷嘴速度与抽吸速度等参数，确定采集头流场最优参数组合。DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.1003-2029.2022.04.006。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Published in the Journal of Ocean Technology (Vol. 41, No. 4, 2022). The internal flow field of a double-row nozzle jet acquisition head is numerically simulated with the standard k-ε turbulence model, matching jet angles, nozzle velocities and suction velocity to determine the optimal parameter combination. DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.1003-2029.2022.04.006.</p>
