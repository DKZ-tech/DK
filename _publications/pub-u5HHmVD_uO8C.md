<p class="lang-zh">发表于《海洋技术学报》2021 年第 40 卷第 5 期。系统比较了机械式、水力式及复合式等深海多金属结核采集方式的工作原理与结构参数，分析了各方式的适用条件与优缺点。DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.1003-2029.2021.05.008。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Published in the Journal of Ocean Technology (Vol. 40, No. 5, 2021). Collection methods for deep-sea polymetallic nodules (mechanical, hydraulic and hybrid) are systematically compared in terms of working principles and structural parameters, analyzing the applicable conditions, advantages and disadvantages of each. DOI: 10.3969/j.issn.1003-2029.2021.05.008.</p>
