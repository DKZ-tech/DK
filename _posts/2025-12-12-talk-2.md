我受邀请到上海国家会展中心参加“春晖计划”三十周年纪念暨2025春晖中国留学人员创新创业交流活动。我的成果作为新能源与绿色科技领域的优秀成果，个人被授予奖牌和奖状，并且可以挑选3个中国城市进行考察调研。感谢教育部的国际机票资助和上海市政府部门的全程支持。<br>
I was invited to the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) to attend the 30th anniversary commemoration of the "Chunhui Program" and the 2025 Chunhui Chinese Overseas Students' Innovation and Entrepreneurship Exchange Activity. My achievement was recognized as an outstanding result in the field of new energy and green technology. I was awarded a medal and a certificate, and I was also allowed to select three Chinese cities for investigation and research. Thank you to the Ministry of Education for the international air ticket subsidy and the full support of the Shanghai Municipal Government departments.
