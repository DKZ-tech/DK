<p class="lang-zh">经学校评审，我获得 2025–2026 年度大连理工大学"连理全球"留学专项资助资格，将以联合培养博士生身份赴卢森堡大学开展为期 6 个月（2026 年 8 月至 2027 年 2 月）的访学。资助包括一次性国际旅费及签证费 1.8 万元人民币、留学期间奖学金生活费（参照国家公派留学人员奖学金标准执行，约 EUR 1350/月）。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Following the university's review, I was awarded the funding qualification for the DUT Global Vision Project for the 2025–2026 academic year. I will undertake a 6-month joint PhD research visit (August 2026 – February 2027) at the University of Luxembourg. The funding package includes a lump-sum international travel and visa fee of RMB 18,000, plus a scholarship living allowance of approximately EUR 1,350/month in accordance with state-sponsored overseas student standards.</p>
