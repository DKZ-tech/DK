<p class="lang-zh">今天，我方接待了印度尼西亚万隆理工学院（ITB）化学工程项目主任 Dr. C.B. Rasrendra 教授，以及英国阿伯丁大学翟恒来博士生来访。来宾一行参观了实验室，并在午餐交流中就相关研究方向与合作进行了深入沟通。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Today we welcomed Prof. Dr. C.B. Rasrendra (Head of the Chemical Engineering Program, Institut Teknologi Bandung, Indonesia) and PhD student Henglai Zhai from the University of Aberdeen. The visit included a laboratory tour and in-depth discussions over lunch on research directions and future collaboration.</p>
