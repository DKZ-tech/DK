今日，卢森堡大学 Bradley Ladewig 博士以及阿伯丁大学张冶水教授和 Henry Tan 博士到访我所在的实验室。此次来访源于双方课题组联合获批的国际合作研究项目。我很荣幸参与此次访问，并与各位学者进行了交流。<br>
Today, Dr. Bradley Ladewig (University of Luxembourg) and Prof. Yeshui Zhang and Dr. Henry Tan (University of Aberdeen) visited our laboratory. This visit was initiated based on an international collaborative research project jointly awarded to both research groups. I was honored to participate in the visit and to have the opportunity to interact with the visiting scholars.
