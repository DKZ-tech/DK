<p class="lang-zh">我受邀请到上海国家会展中心参加“春晖计划”三十周年纪念暨2025春晖中国留学人员创新创业交流活动。我的成果作为新能源与绿色科技领域的优秀成果，个人被授予奖牌和奖状，并且可以挑选3个中国城市进行考察调研。感谢教育部的国际机票资助和上海市政府部门的全程支持。</p>
<p class="lang-en">I was invited to the National Exhibition and Convention Center (Shanghai) to attend the 30th anniversary commemoration of the "Chunhui Program" and the 2025 Chunhui Innovation and Entrepreneurship Exchange Event for Chinese Overseas Students. My achievement was recognized as an outstanding result in the field of new energy and green technology; I was awarded a medal and a certificate, and was invited to visit three Chinese cities for research tours. My thanks go to the Ministry of Education for the international travel support and to the Shanghai municipal authorities for their generous assistance.</p>
