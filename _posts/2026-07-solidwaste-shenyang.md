<p class="lang-zh">我参加了在沈阳举办的2026年固体废物绿色发展交流会（中华环保联合会主办），并作了题为「大型危险废物回转窑焚烧过程分级配风与协同处置多目标优化研究」的口头报告。</p>
<p class="lang-en">I attended the 2026 Green Development Exchange on Solid Waste in Shenyang, China (organized by the All-China Environmental Federation), where I delivered an oral presentation titled "Multi-objective Optimization of Staged Air Distribution and Co-disposal in a Large-scale Hazardous Waste Rotary Kiln Incineration Process."</p>
