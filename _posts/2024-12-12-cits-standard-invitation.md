<p class="lang-zh">中国检验检测学会发函邀请大连理工大学作为《基于高温热解气化的垃圾制氢技术要求》团体标准的起草单位，姬国钊教授与我作为起草人参与标准起草与研讨工作。</p>
<p class="lang-en">The China Inspection and Testing Society (CITS) officially invited Dalian University of Technology to serve as the drafting organization for the group standard "Technical Requirements for Hydrogen Production from Waste Based on High-Temperature Pyrolysis and Gasification" (T/CITS 242-2025). Prof. Guozhao Ji and I joined the standard drafting and discussion as named drafters.</p>
