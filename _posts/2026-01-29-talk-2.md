今天，我方接待了印度尼西亚万隆理工学院（ITB）化学工程项目主任 Dr. C.B. Rasrendra 教授，以及英国阿伯丁大学翟恒来博士生来访。来宾一行参观了实验室，并在午餐交流中就相关研究方向与合作进行了深入沟通。<br>
Today, we welcomed Professor Dr. C.B. Rasrendra of Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB) and PhD student Zhai Henglai from the University of Aberdeen. The visit included a laboratory tour and discussions on research topics and collaboration during lunch.
