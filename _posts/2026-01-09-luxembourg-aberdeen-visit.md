<p class="lang-zh">今日，卢森堡大学 Bradley Ladewig 教授以及阿伯丁大学张冶水教授和 Henry Tan 教授等人到访我所在的实验室。此次来访源于双方课题组联合获批的国际合作研究项目。我很荣幸参与此次访问，并与各位学者进行了交流。</p>
<p class="lang-en">Prof. Bradley Ladewig (University of Luxembourg), together with Prof. Yeshui Zhang and Prof. Henry Tan (University of Aberdeen), visited our laboratory today. The visit was initiated through an international collaborative research project jointly awarded to both research groups. I was honored to take part and to exchange ideas with the visiting scholars.</p>
