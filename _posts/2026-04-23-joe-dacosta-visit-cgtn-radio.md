<p class="lang-zh">2026 年 4 月 17 日至 23 日，我导师的导师、澳大利亚昆士兰大学化工学院 <a href="https://chemeng.uq.edu.au/profile/7851/joe-diniz-da-costa">Joe Diniz da Costa</a> 教授来访大连理工大学环境学院。期间，Joe 教授与课题组围绕膜分离、结晶机理与工业过程建模等方向展开深入交流，并做客 CGTN RADIO 演播室，就清洁能源与环境技术话题参与了一场访谈。</p>
<p class="lang-en">From April 17 to 23, 2026, <a href="https://chemeng.uq.edu.au/profile/7851/joe-diniz-da-costa">Prof. Joe Diniz da Costa</a> from the School of Chemical Engineering at The University of Queensland — academic mentor of my PhD supervisor — visited the School of Environmental Science and Engineering at Dalian University of Technology. He exchanged ideas with our group on membrane separation, crystallization mechanisms and industrial process modeling, and joined a CGTN RADIO studio interview on clean energy and environmental technologies.</p>
