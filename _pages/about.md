我叫张东宽，是大连理工大学环境学院的博士生，我的导师是姬国钊副教授。（[导师资料](https://faculty.dlut.edu.cn/jiguozhao/zh_CN/index.htm)）。
我于2025年，在东京科学大学（前东京工业大学）进行联合培养，为期一年。在东京科学大学期间，我在Cross实验室工作，接受Jeffrey S. Cross教授的指导和培养。（[实验室网站](https://zh.clab-tokyotech.org/)）。<br>
My name is Dongkuan Zhang, and I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Environmental Science and Engineering, Dalian University of Technology. I am supervised by Associate Professor Guozhao Ji ([Profile](https://faculty.dlut.edu.cn/jiguozhao/zh_CN/index.htm)).
In 2025, I am a visiting Ph.D. student at the Institute of Science Tokyo (formerly Tokyo Institute of Technology) for a one-year joint Ph.D. program. During my stay, I work in the Cross Laboratory under the supervision of Professor Jeffrey S. Cross ([Lab website](https://zh.clab-tokyotech.org/)).

我的研究方向是基于智能化多相流建模的废弃物焚烧技术。内容包括：CFD数值模拟、人工智能（AI）流场预测、设备流场设计与优化。目前，我正在开发用于蒸馏残渣燃烧的焚烧炉智能多相流模型，这项研究将数值模拟与人工智能技术相结合，具有广阔的应用前景。<br>
My research focuses on intelligent multiphase flow modeling for waste incineration technologies. This work includes CFD-based numerical simulations, AI-driven flow field prediction, and flow field design and optimization of incineration equipment.
Currently, I am developing an intelligent multiphase flow model for the combustion of distillation residues, which integrates numerical simulations with artificial intelligence techniques and shows broad application potential.

I aim to advance cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable industrial processes by integrating physics-based computational modeling with AI-driven flow field prediction.<br>
