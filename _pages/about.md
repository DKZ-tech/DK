<h2><span class="lang-zh">研究方向</span><span class="lang-en">Research Interests</span></h2>

<p class="lang-zh">我的研究方向是<strong>面向废弃物焚烧技术的智能多相流建模</strong>，将基于物理机理的 CFD 数值模拟与人工智能驱动的流场预测相结合。目前，我正在开发用于蒸馏残渣燃烧的焚烧炉智能多相流模型，致力于推动工业过程向更加清洁、智能和可持续的方向发展。</p>
<p class="lang-en">My research focuses on <strong>intelligent multiphase flow modeling for waste incineration technologies</strong>, combining physics-based CFD simulation with AI-driven flow field prediction. Currently, I am developing an intelligent multiphase flow model for the combustion of distillation residues in incinerators, aiming to advance cleaner, smarter and more sustainable industrial processes.</p>
